# The web pages' artwork

The pictures behind the pages in `web/pages` (each page's `hero.jpg` is made from the one in its
folder here), and the drafts and series that aren't used but are kept. All were generated by the
author -- with ChatGPT (ChatGPT Pro), the three in `not-used/victorian-workshop` with Google Gemini;
the rights in them are the author's.

Names: `<style>-<subject>-<scene>.<ext>`, lowercase, words joined by hyphens. A page's picture is in
the page's folder; one that isn't used is in `not-used/<page it was drafted for>/`, or in
`not-used/<series>/` when no page has its style.
