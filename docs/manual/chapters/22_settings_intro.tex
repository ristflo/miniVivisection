Every setting is in two places: the \vn{Settings} page in the Script Editor's left panel (applied at once; text fields when
you press \key{Enter} or leave the box), and \menu{Tools\>Options\>miniVivisection}, in collapsible groups, each setting
with a tooltip -- on three tabs: \emph{General}, what the Script Editor and Grasshopper's script components share
(and \emph{Markdown}: MarkdownText, \vn{Text} and Panels alike); \emph{Rhino}, what happens in Rhino (watches drawn in the
viewports, selection, the \code{RunPythonScript} command, FreePause); \emph{Grasshopper}, script components, Panels and
the prompts window. They are kept between Rhino sessions. A setting marked \emph{(new windows)} applies to Script Editors
opened afterwards.

The two switches in the Script Editor's top bar, \vn{Debug} and \vn{AI}, aren't listed here (\see{sec:vnames}). While
\vn{Debug} is off, the debugging features read as off whatever their settings say, and come back as they were.
